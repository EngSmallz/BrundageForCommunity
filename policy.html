Policy & Priorities

Practical solutions focused on budget transparency, digital privacy, and community-first governance.

01

Fiscal Transparency & Budget Accountability

The public budget should be completely understandable by the people it affects. Budget responsibility is a shared community effort, and the residents who fund public services deserve to know their hard-earned dollars are being utilized with peak efficiency.

  • ✓ Plain-language financial reporting
  • ✓ Direct oversight of capital expenses
  • ✓ Open accounting for taxpayers
02

Local Autonomy & Housing Growth

We need less bureaucratic regulation to allow for sensible housing expansion and increased utility availability. Community input must always come first to ensure growth serves residents while eliminating unnecessary administrative friction.

  • ✓ Streamlined property & housing regulations
  • ✓ Reliable utility infrastructure planning
  • ✓ Resident-first decision making
03

Educational Support & Student Retention

Strengthening our educational system requires creative benefit options for staff alongside pathways that keep students engaged. We must build opportunities that help students discover their true passions right here in their home district.

  • ✓ Innovative staff retention initiatives
  • ✓ Hands-on technical & trade pathways
  • ✓ Modern learning environment tools
04

Digital Privacy & Public Usability

Municipal and educational systems should leverage technology to make life easier—not more invasive. I support fee-free online payment options, easy-to-use public websites, and strong privacy protections against open AI-driven automated surveillance.

  • ✓ Zero-fee digital payment portals
  • ✓ Opposing automated public surveillance
  • ✓ Accessible, intuitive government sites
